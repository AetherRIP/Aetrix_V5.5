## Notes from the review

- Q19's printed answer contains the product `5xy`. The SymPy scheme check reads
  `xy` as a single symbol, so a comparison there would be wrong rather than silent;
  the check is skipped for Q19 and the model's answer is reviewed by a person.
- The two `M1` points of Q18, Q23, Q26, Q27, Q30, Q31, Q32, Q34, Q35, Q36 and Q37a
  are a method mark and its follow-through. Their dependency is recorded as data, so
  the verifier can reject a follow-through award made without the first method mark.
- Q13 carries a printed prose alternative (factorise first) rather than a
  mark-point code; it describes the same route and adds no marks.
- Q16(d) is printed on scheme page 6 with 2 marks, but the blueprint carries only
  `16b`. The criteria therefore total the paper's 89 scoreable marks, not the
  scheme's 91 printed ones; the exclusion is listed in
  `experiment/supervised_pilot/excluded_scheme_entries.csv`.
- Q5 and Q14 print typographical slips in their tolerance cells. The intended
  expression is recorded and the printed text is kept in `source_quote`; both are
  described in the payload's extraction notes.
- An independent audit of the printed pages corrected seven transcription faults
  (Q1d, Q4, Q6, Q13, Q19, Q27, Q34). Each correction is pinned by
  `backend/tests/unit/test_pilot_scheme_criteria.py`, which fails if a printed
  tolerance is dropped or an unprinted one is added.
- A coded alternative (Q34 `M2`, Q36 `M2`/`B2`, Q37a `B2`) records the codes it
  replaces, so the verifier can refuse an award that earns the route and the marks
  it stands in for. A route whose scope is missing, undefined or narrower than its
  own prerequisites is refused before any paper is marked.
- The two no-working routes print `3 out of a maximum of 4 terms`. Each records the
  expression and its own threshold, which is counted deterministically; an answer
  that cannot be read as a sum of terms is reported for review and never treated as
  satisfying the threshold.
- Q6, Q11, Q13, Q28 and Q37b print two independent mark points under one code. A
  reply that awards that code once names neither of them, so the verifier reports
  `AMBIGUOUS_CRITERION_CODE` for a person to attribute; a full award of both points
  is not flagged. Where the second point is a follow-through, the dependency makes
  a single award the earlier point and no ambiguity is reported.
